1 The basics
Machine identities & secrets
A plain-language guide

Most of the logins in your
systems aren't people.

Apps, services, scripts and AI tools log in constantly to do their jobs. Each one needs a secret to prove who it is — and they far outnumber the people who work there.

Applications Cloud workloads CI/CD pipelines Scripts & jobs Service accounts AI agents

A machine identity

Any piece of software that needs to authenticate — an app, a service, a pipeline, an AI agent. Often called a non-human identity.

A secret

The password, key, token or certificate it uses to log in. Lose control of one and someone can act as that system.

Why it's a blind spot

They multiply faster than anyone tracks, and most organisations have no full list of how many exist or who owns them.

3 Why it matters

What good looks like
for the business.

Fewer ways in for attackers

Stolen credentials are the most common way intruders get in. Managing secrets well quietly closes that door.

Audits you can answer fast

Show regulators clear evidence in minutes instead of scrambling for weeks before each review.

No paying twice

A clear picture stops overlapping tools quietly running in parallel — and the duplicate bills that come with them.

Fewer surprise outages

Automatic renewal means systems don't break when a forgotten certificate or password quietly expires.

Good secrets management is mostly invisible — you notice it most when it's missing.
