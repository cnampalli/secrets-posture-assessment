   architect reader will catch this immediately.

   should reconcile with the §9 NHI table once #1 above is fixed.

   then "(8 + 8 adjacent)" but lists 8 T1552 + 8 adjacent = 16, matching
   App A/D. Internally fine; just ensure the "16 techniques" figure is
   used consistently (it is in §5, §13, App A, App D).
4. **§14 CPS 234 paragraph normalisation** is stated as "§21(a)-(d),
   §27(a)-(e), §35(a)-(b)" in the body but App A §A.3.1 header writes
   "§21(a)-(d), §27(a)-(e), §35" (drops the (a)-(b)). Cosmetic, but a
   regulator reader notices paragraph citations.
