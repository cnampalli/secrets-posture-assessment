# Posture Assessment — Methodology

**Status:** Draft
**Date:** 2026-05-30
**Audience:** Client — buyer / executive sponsor.
**Internal companion:** `methodology/PLAYBOOK.md`

Our posture assessment measures how well your organisation governs its machine identities
against the frameworks that bind you, and hands you a prioritised, defensible remediation
roadmap. The method is the same for every engagement; what changes is your evidence.

## What the method does

The assessment runs as six stages, in order. We first agree the frameworks your organisation
is held to, then gather honest evidence of what you do today, score each area against a
published standard, and report exactly where you stand. From there we turn the gaps into a
sequenced plan of work and set the cadence to keep the picture current. Each stage produces a
concrete deliverable you keep, and every verdict traces back to the evidence behind it — so the
findings hold up under scrutiny from your own risk function and your regulator. The structure
does not change between engagements, which makes the results comparable over time and across
peers; only your evidence and your obligations differ.

## Scope

We scope the assessment to the frameworks that genuinely bind your industry — the regulatory
and security obligations you are legally or contractually required to meet — confirmed with your
compliance and risk stakeholders rather than assumed from your sector label. On top of that we
layer one or two comparison overlays so you can see yourself against a stricter peer or a
standard you are considering adopting, and we confirm the Australian baseline that applies to
every organisation. Getting scope right is what keeps the assessment precise: broad enough to
cover your real exposure, narrow enough that nothing tested is noise. **You receive** the scoped
framework set that everything downstream is measured against.

## Collect evidence

We facilitate a structured evidence-gathering session, working through each in-scope area with
your subject-matter experts so the answers reflect what you actually do, not an optimistic
self-assessment. Because we run it live, we can probe for the underlying artifact and keep the
record grounded and specific; where you cannot yet answer something, we record that honestly as
an open item rather than guess. Sessions can be paused and resumed, so the work fits around your
people's availability. **You receive** a complete evidence record covering every area in scope.

## Score

Each use-case is scored against a published rubric, so the standard you are measured by is
transparent and the same for everyone. Our assessor then confirms or overrides the rubric's
result using their own judgment — and every override is documented with the reasoning behind it
and a stated confidence level reflecting how strong the evidence is. Nothing is decided
silently: each verdict is traceable both to the published standard and to the assessor's
recorded judgment. **You receive** a transparent, defensible scorecard that withstands challenge
from your risk function and your regulator.

## Report current state

You receive a gap report across all use-cases, each marked Met, Partial, Gap, or Pending against
the framework set we scoped at the start — a faithful, area-by-area view of exactly where you
stand. Nothing new is decided at this stage; it is a clear rendering of the scored verdict into
a report you and your stakeholders can read directly. Gap and Partial areas are the findings
that feed your roadmap; Pending areas flag where evidence was insufficient and a revisit is
owed. **You receive** the current-state report — your defensible picture of today's posture.

## Build the remediation roadmap

Gaps become a prioritised engagement menu using a risk-versus-effort model: we band each finding
by the exposure it carries and the effort to close it, then sequence them so you know what to do
first, why, and what each item depends on. Regulatory obligations escalate priority — where an
obligation makes an exposure non-discretionary, it raises a finding's standing, and we always
record the specific control reference so you can trace why a finding moved. We deliberately use
qualitative bands rather than false-precision scores, and we record sequencing constraints so
the plan is actually buildable in order. **You receive** a sequenced remediation roadmap — an
ordered, dependency-aware plan tied to your obligations.

## Re-assess

A posture assessment is a point-in-time snapshot, so we re-assess on a defined cadence to keep
it current: annually as a standing baseline, out of cycle when a significant event such as an
incident, a major change, or a new obligation warrants it, and after each remediation closes. The
per-finding re-test is the evidence that a remediation actually worked — a finding is not
considered closed until its re-test confirms the state has moved. **You receive** measured
progress over time: the area-by-area changes that show how your posture improved since the last
assessment.

## The value

The assessment is built to reduce risk you can otherwise only guess at. By surfacing where
machine-identity credentials are exposed — and prioritising the live exposures first — it lowers
both the likelihood and the impact of a credential compromise before it becomes an incident.
The value is cost-avoidance and exposure-reduction: closing the gaps that an attacker would
exploit, in the order that retires the most risk soonest, rather than spending evenly across
problems that do not all carry the same weight.

It also leaves you audit-ready. Because every verdict is scored against a published standard and
traced to its evidence, the current-state report and roadmap are defensible against the
frameworks that bind you — APRA, the ASD ISM, and the Essential 8 — so you can demonstrate to
your regulator not only where you stand today but that you have a credible, prioritised plan to
improve. Re-assessment turns that one-time snapshot into a documented record of progress, giving
you evidence that the remediation work landed and that your posture is being actively governed
rather than left to decay.
